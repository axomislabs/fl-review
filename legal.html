Contact form

In this draft the contact form does not transmit anything to a server: the entries are assembled locally in your browser and handed to your email program. Once a backend is connected, this section must describe where the data goes, how long it is kept and on what legal basis.

Review and comment function

The comment layer used for reviewing this draft stores comments exclusively in your own browser (localStorage). Nothing is transmitted automatically. Data leaves your device only when you actively choose to download the CSV file or open the pre-filled email — and then only to the recipient you send it to.

A comment can also be dictated instead of typed. The microphone runs only while you have switched the Dictate button on, and this website neither records nor stores the audio — it receives only the transcribed text, in the comment field you see. The recognition itself is done by your browser: Chrome and Edge send the audio to the browser vendor's speech recognition service while recording, Safari uses the recognition built into the operating system. If dictation stays on the live site, this paragraph needs to be confirmed against the browser vendors' current terms.

Cookies and analytics

This draft sets no cookies and loads no external resources — no fonts, scripts, maps or analytics from third-party servers. The only connection to a third party this site can cause is the browser's own speech recognition, and only while you are dictating a comment. If analytics or embedded services are added later, a consent banner and the corresponding section will be required.

Your rights

Standard wording on access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
