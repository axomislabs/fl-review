Benchmark case

Twelve years unchallenged, and one phone call

The engagement this business is built to repeat. Told without names: the client is identified by sector and size, the vendors are not identified at all.

The client
A Mexican retail group — a listed business in the billions, comparable to a large German department-store chain.
Challenge
The platform delivering their entire web shop — content delivery, web application firewall, API and DDoS protection — had run on the same vendor for twelve years. Nobody had evaluated it in that time. The contract stood at € 2.4m a year and had never been benchmarked against the market.
Approach
A short technical review gave us their real usage metrics. We asked a competing provider to quote against exactly those metrics — like for like, no assumptions — and the offer came back at roughly half the incumbent price.
Result
The client took that number to their existing vendor and renegotiated. € 500,000 a year came off the contract — € 1.5m across the three-year term. No tender, no migration, no change of provider.
✎
