Why this is hard

In our assessment, around 95 % of companies are making avoidable mistakes in what they buy — either paying well over the market, or leaving holes open.

Buying technology is not like buying anything else a procurement team handles. There is a technical judgement sitting underneath every commercial one, and the market moves faster than any single buyer can stay current with.

  • It is a technical purchase before it is a commercial one You cannot negotiate a contract you cannot read. Comparing two offers means comparing two architectures, two operating models and two sets of hidden limits.
  • The market outruns the buyer Pricing models, vendor positions and whole product categories move within a quarter. Large language models alone created security requirements that were on nobody's list two years ago.
  • There are two kinds of mistake, and most firms only measure one Paying too much is visible in a budget. Being exposed is not — which is why it usually goes unpriced until it is expensive.

What we do

Procurement expertise you would otherwise have to hire

We support companies in the selection, procurement and negotiation of software and technology solutions — either as a complete outsourced procurement function, or alongside your existing team for a single strategic deal.

Three comparable offers, non-binding

We bring you credible alternative quotes for what you already run. You do not have to switch and you do not have to migrate — but you can put them on the table at the next negotiation.

Market price & terms benchmarking

Vendor comparison from your side of the table

We compare against your requirement, not against a vendor's roadmap. How we are paid — and by whom — is set out in plain terms further down this page.

Functional & technical advisory

We explain where products really differ under the hood — architecture, coverage, operational burden — not just what the datasheet claims.

Proof

Twelve years unchallenged, and one phone call

A Mexican retail group — a listed business in the billions — had run the same content delivery and web security platform for twelve years. In all that time the contract had never been benchmarked. They were paying € 2.4m a year.

  • A short technical review gave us their real usage metrics.
  • We asked a competing provider to quote against exactly those metrics. The offer came back at roughly half.
  • The client took that number to their incumbent and renegotiated. No tender, no migration, no switch.

How we are paid

There is no price list. Your success is our success — we keep a share of the savings we actually help you realise.

Nothing to budget up front

No day rates and no fixed project price. You do not have to get a spend approved before you find out whether there is money on the table.

A share of what you save

We keep a percentage of the direct savings on the contracts we were involved in — and only on those. No saving, no invoice.

And from the delivery side

Where a client decides to move, the implementation partner who does the work also compensates us. We say so whenever that applies to your case.

✎
This section needs a legal check before launch. A success share plus payment from the delivery side is a defensible model, but in Germany advertising advice as independent while taking undisclosed vendor-side compensation is attackable as misleading. The disclosure wording above, and the percentage itself, should be reviewed by a lawyer.
How it works

A procurement process that stands up to audit

Four stages, fixed deliverables, and a documented decision trail you can hand to finance, security and legal.

Requirements & baseline

We capture the technical requirement, map your current contracts, spend and usage, and agree what a good outcome looks like in numbers.

Market scan & shortlist

We approach the relevant vendors, structure the RFI/RFP, and reduce the field to a comparable shortlist with a like-for-like commercial model.

Negotiation & counter-offers

We negotiate price, commit, term, SLA and exit conditions, keeping competitive tension alive until the last round.

Award & handover

Signed contract, documented rationale, tracked savings, and a renewal calendar so the same conversation is easier next time.
